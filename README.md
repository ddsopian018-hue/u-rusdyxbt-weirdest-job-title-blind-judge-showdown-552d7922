One weird job title (54 characters): **"Senior Vice President of Microwave Beep Interpretation"**. The technique follows the judge brief: a real-sounding corporate rank — Senior Vice President — plus exactly one gloriously specific twist, interpreting microwave beeps. Everyone knows the beep; nobody has met the executive who decodes it. The title reads instantly, pictures a person at a desk solemnly listening to a kitchen appliance, and lands its joke on the first read.
